# Phase 6 — Source Comparison Notes

## Scope

This is a concise comparison artifact for the source strategy audit. It is intentionally conservative and limited to sources that are relevant to ACE’s actual business need: construction opportunities that can eventually become estimating and outreach work.

## Summary

| SOURCE | KIND | GOOD FOR | MAIN LIMITATION | RECOMMENDATION |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| USAspending | award / federal spending data | development, supplemental market signals, public data | not necessarily active bid opportunities | supplement only |
| SAM.gov opportunities | federal procurement notices | active federal opportunities | may not cover local construction needs well enough | strong candidate for public-source expansion |
| State procurement portals | local public solicitations | local construction work | platform inconsistency | likely strong long-term path |
| Local government portals | local public bids | city/county construction opportunities | site policy and format variance | strong candidate |
| ConstructConnect | commercial project database | active opportunity richness | paid; licensing required | future commercial source |
| Dodge / PlanHub / Blue Book | commercial bid networks | opportunity volume and bid details | paid; licensing required | future commercial source |

## Public-source direction

The current repo is intentionally $0 and local-first. That means the most sensible public-source strategy is:
- keep the current USAspending-style fixture as a development and supplemental source
- treat SAM.gov and official local/state government procurement portals as the next public-source candidates
- postpone commercial feed integration until ACE has a verified business case and a contract

## Important distinction

Public federal award data is useful, but it is not equivalent to active bid opportunities. ACE needs active construction opportunities with deadlines, scope detail, and project lifecycle clarity before it can drive contractor matching and outreach effectively.
